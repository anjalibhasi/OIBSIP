WHO HE WAS

A Life Dedicated to India

Scientist

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam played an important role in India's space and missile development programmes and became one of India's most respected scientists.

President

He served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007 and was widely known as the People's President.

Visionary

Kalam dedicated much of his life to inspiring students and encouraging young people to dream, learn and contribute to India's future.

HIS JOURNEY

Milestones of His Life

1931

Born in Rameswaram

A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

1960

Joined DRDO

He began his career as a scientist at the Defence Research and Development Organisation.

1969

Joined ISRO

Kalam became involved in India's space programme and worked on important launch vehicle projects.

1980s

Missile Development

He played a major role in India's Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme.

2002

President of India

He became the 11th President of India and served until 2007.

2015

A Lasting Legacy

Dr. Kalam passed away on 27 July 2015 while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong.
