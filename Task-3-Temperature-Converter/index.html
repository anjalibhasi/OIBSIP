CONVERSION FORMULAS

Understand the Math Behind It.

Temperature conversion uses simple mathematical formulas to move accurately between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.

Celsius → Fahrenheit (°C × 9/5) + 32
Fahrenheit → Celsius (°F − 32) × 5/9
Celsius → Kelvin °C + 273.15
Kelvin → Celsius K − 273.15
